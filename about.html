I’m Jason King. I build sales infrastructure for high-ticket businesses — so revenue can scale without the founder living inside every deal.

The pattern was consistent: I could sell and lead almost anywhere. What broke at scale wasn’t talent. It was reliance on heroics instead of systems.

That gap is why I founded Argos. Argos Consult and Argos Revenue Command help operators install the people, process, and tools so revenue runs with clarity, accountability, and less founder dependency.
